## Why
Save, move, delete, and upload results use Material snackbars on every platform. Feedback should fit the platform and remain visible when deletion closes the note.

## What changes
Share a feedback presenter with compact desktop notifications, inline iOS status bars, and native Android snackbars. Include dismiss controls, accessible announcements, and reduced-motion support.

## Impact
Limited to result feedback for note actions and file uploads. Conflict and lock interfaces continue to show their existing dedicated status.
